# Content Source Rules

Use the corporate documents and approved company information as the authoritative source for factual company content.

Before publication, verify all:

- Legal company details
- Contact details
- Expert names and titles
- Expert biographies
- Client names
- Project names
- Project results
- Certifications
- Partnerships
- Awards
- Locations
- Statistics

Do not publish unsupported or fabricated information.

The website will support French and English. Maintain equivalent meaning between both languages.
